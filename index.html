Trade Journal ↗

Built Mar 2026 · on GitLab since Oct 2026

My trading felt inconsistent, and I wanted to know why.

So I log every trade with its setup and tag the mistake if I made one, and the R multiple works itself out as I type. The stats show which setups hold up and which mistakes keep creeping in, so the inconsistency stops being a guess. Trades save to my own Google Sheet and sync when I am back online. The link opens it with sample trades.

Equarian Markets ↗

Built Jul 2026 · on GitLab since Oct 2026 · 45 indicators · 4 models

Before I trade micro futures I want rates, credit, vol and yen carry on one screen, read the way a desk would read them.

It pulls free data from FRED, Yahoo, the CFTC and the BOJ. In the browser it runs four models: a risk appetite index, a growth and inflation regime map, the absorption ratio for how fragile markets are, and the NY Fed's recession model. A daily read spells out what stands out, like oil and grains rising while stocks fall, with the numbers behind it. A portfolio tracker pulls my positions from four brokers into one view. An alt signals tab keeps the market-wide curiosities: the Pentagon Pizza Index, the Stripper Index and the moon, each tested against the index to say whether it has ever held up.

  • JavaScript
  • Vite
  • Cloudflare Pages
  • PWA

Oracular ↗

Built Sep 2026 · on GitLab since Oct 2026

I wanted the read a desk would write on one ticker, every number on the page and nothing taken on faith.

Any US ticker, read off live data: a Council of twelve seats votes on the numbers, an AI writes the plain-English memo and argues both sides, and seasonality, derivatives and levels each show their work. There's a Market tab too, with a Fear & Greed index built from seven indicators, plus a chat that answers with the ticker's own data in front of it.

  • React
  • Vite
  • Tailwind
  • Cloudflare Workers
  • Mistral AI

Tally

Built Oct 2026 · 36-hour hackathon · TOKEN2049 Origins

A trust layer for business deals. Buyers and sellers who don't know each other agree on the evidence first. Funds wait in escrow on Cardano until that evidence arrives. Every finished deal updates a reliability record for both sides.

Built with teammates for the TOKEN2049 Origins hackathon. Contracts with milestones, tiered disputes, Masumi escrow on Cardano preprod with test USDM, and AI Coworkers that draft terms, rulings, and trust checks. It runs on preprod, so nothing has real value.

  • TypeScript
  • React
  • Cardano
  • Masumi
