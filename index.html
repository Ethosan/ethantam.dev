Economics, 2027

The first stock I ever bought was a gold ETF, at 14, in the middle of COVID. I mostly wanted to understand why everyone was suddenly piling into gold. That question turned into a habit of reading about how fear, incentives and institutions move markets, which is roughly how I ended up choosing Economics.

At school I co-founded the ACSians Investment Club with some friends: workshops for beginners and stock pitches, plus talks from people working in the industry. For my Extended Essay I tried predicting bitcoin prices with machine learning, which taught me a lot about messy data and about how humbling prediction is.

I started building apps because I wanted tools that didn't exist yet, like a money tracker that understands PayLater and splitting bills with friends. A couple of them are below.

Projects

Trade Journal ↗

Built Mar 2026 · on GitHub since Oct 2026

My futures account kept going down, and I wanted to know why.

So I log every trade with its setup and any rule I broke, and it works out the R multiple as I type. The stats gave me the answer: the trades where I broke a rule lost more than the clean ones made. Trades save to my own Google Sheet, and it still works offline. The link opens it with sample trades.

  • Vite

Equarian Markets ↗

Built Jul 2026 · on GitHub since Oct 2026 · 36 indicators · 4 models

Before I trade micro futures I want rates, credit, vol and yen carry on one screen, read the way a desk would read them.

It pulls free data from FRED, Yahoo, the CFTC and the BOJ. In the browser it runs four models: a risk appetite index, a growth and inflation regime map, the absorption ratio for how fragile markets are, and the NY Fed's recession model. A daily read spells out what stands out, like oil and grains rising while stocks fall, with the numbers behind it.

  • JavaScript
  • Vite
  • Netlify
  • PWA

The List ↗

Built Aug 2026

My friends and I kept forgetting where we'd eaten and where we still wanted to go.

So now there's a shared list. We rate places and keep a leaderboard. When someone adds a place to the want list, it goes and finds a good TikTok and the details by itself. It also lives in our Telegram group as a bot.
