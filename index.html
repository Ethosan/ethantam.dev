01 / About

The first stock I ever bought was a gold ETF, at 14, in the middle of COVID. I mostly wanted to understand why everyone was suddenly piling into gold. That question turned into a habit of reading about how fear, incentives and institutions move markets, which is roughly how I ended up choosing Economics.

At school I co-founded the ACSians Investment Club with some friends: workshops for beginners, stock pitches, and talks from people working in the industry. For my Extended Essay I tried predicting bitcoin prices with machine learning, which taught me a lot about messy data and about how humbling prediction is.

I started building apps because I wanted tools that didn't exist yet, like a money tracker that understands PayLater and splitting bills with friends. A couple of them are below.

20bucks

Since Sep 2026 · 167 commits

I wanted one place to see where my money actually goes, including PayLater instalments and the "I'll pay you back" bills that never show up in a banking app.

It grew into a proper finance app: bill splits, trading P&L, statement imports, and it works offline. Plain JavaScript and Supabase, plus a small AI check that flags entries that look off.

The List

Since Aug 2026 · 143 commits

My friends and I kept forgetting where we'd eaten and where we still wanted to go.

So now there's a shared list. We rate places, there's a leaderboard, and when someone adds a place to the want list it goes and finds a good TikTok and the details by itself. It also lives in our Telegram group as a bot. TypeScript on Cloudflare Workers, with Supabase.
